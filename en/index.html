Count your pulse

Find your pulse on the wrist and tap the strip on every beat. This isn’t a diagnosis — if your pulse worries you, book a visit.

What people see a cardiologist about

  • 01High or fluctuating blood pressure
  • 02Skipped beats or a racing heart
  • 03Chest pain or pressure
  • 04Breathlessness, swollen ankles
  • 05Cholesterol, blood sugar, heart attacks in the family
  • 06Dizziness and fainting
  • 07Several medicines at once
  • 08A check before surgery or sport

Severe chest pain lasting more than a few minutes, sudden breathlessness or fainting — call 112, don’t wait for an appointment.
